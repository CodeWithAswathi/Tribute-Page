Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs and inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

He believed strongly in education, innovation, and the power of youth to transform the nation. His contributions to science and society continue to inspire people around the world.
